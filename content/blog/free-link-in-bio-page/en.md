# A free link-in-bio page on yoy.fyi

Instagram gives your profile one link. You usually have more than one thing to point at: events, a shop, a sign-up form. That's what link-in-bio pages are for. One address, a list of buttons.

On yoy.fyi that page is free and lives at `yoy.fyi/@your-name`.

## How to set it up

1. Sign in to yoy.fyi with Google and create a few short links.
2. Open the bio page section from the top menu.
3. Pick an address: 3 to 30 characters, lowercase Latin letters, digits, dot, hyphen or underscore.
4. Add a title and a short description if you like.
5. Tick the links that belong on the page, up to 30, and save.

The page is public right away, with the newest links on top.

## What it does

Every button goes through a yoy.fyi short link, so clicks from the page land in the usual stats. You can see which button works and which countries and devices the taps come from.

A link you disable, or one that expires, drops off the page on its own. No more stale announcements sitting in your bio.

The page title and description show up in the preview when someone pastes the address into a chat.

## From a chat

If yoy.fyi is connected to Claude or ChatGPT, you can build the page with words: "Make me a bio page @lvivgigs with my three latest concert links." The assistant calls the `update_bio` tool and gives you the address.

## What it doesn't have

Themes, avatars and embedded videos. The page is plain: a title, a description and buttons. For most bios that's enough, and it loads fast even on a weak mobile connection.
